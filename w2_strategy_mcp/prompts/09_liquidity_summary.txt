# W2 Strategy MCP - Liquidity Summary Prompt

Let me put together your complete liquidity picture.

**Your Available Liquidity:** [TOTAL SOURCES]
- Cash accounts: [DEPOSITS AMOUNT]
- Brokerage cash: [BROKERAGE CASH]
- Available credit: [CREDIT AMOUNT]
[IF HOMEOWNER WITH HELOC] - HELOC availability: [HELOC AMOUNT]

**Your Liquidity Needs:** [TOTAL USES]
- Emergency fund target: [MONTHLY EXPENSES] × [TARGET MONTHS] = [BASE AMOUNT]
- Additional scenarios: [OTHER SCENARIOS TOTAL]

**Bottom Line:** You have a [SURPLUS/DEFICIT] of [AMOUNT] in liquidity.

[IF SURPLUS] This means you're in good shape liquidity-wise, and might even consider whether some of that cash could be working harder for you elsewhere.

[IF DEFICIT] This suggests you might want to build up more accessible liquidity, either through saving more cash or expanding your credit facilities.

**Three Key Liquidity Risks to Keep in Mind:**

1. **Stacked Events:** Multiple things hitting at once - like losing your job during a market downturn when your home also needs major repairs. Your [HIGH/MODERATE/LOW] economic exposure makes this [more/less] likely.

2. **Timing Issues:** Needing cash when your other sources aren't accessible - like credit markets tightening during economic stress, or not being able to access a HELOC when you need it most.

3. **Opportunity Costs:** Having too much in cash when you could be earning better returns, vs. having too little when you need flexibility.

The goal is finding the right balance for your specific economic exposure profile and comfort level.

---

## Calculation Logic:

**Total Sources:**
deposits_amount + brokerage_cash_amount + available_credit_amount + heloc_amount

**Total Uses:**
(monthly_spending_estimate × emergency_budget_months) + additional_scenarios

**Surplus/Deficit:**
liquidity_surplus_deficit = Total Sources - Total Uses

**Risk Personalization:**
- HIGH economic exposure: "more likely" + emphasize stacked events
- LOW economic exposure: "less likely" + focus on efficiency
- HOMEOWNER: Include property-related risks
- RENTER: Focus on job/health/transportation scenarios
