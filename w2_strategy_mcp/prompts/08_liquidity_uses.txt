That's a clear picture of your sources. Now, let's think about the other side of the equation: potential uses for that liquidity.

Most financial advice focuses only on income replacement after a job loss. But life is more complex. Consider a scenario where in the same year you have to:
- Cover a large medical deductible.
- Pay for a major home repair that insurance doesn't fully cover.
- Replace a car after an accident, wanting something nicer than the insurance payout.
- Help a family member in a crisis.

This isn't to be alarming, but to be realistic. A robust liquidity plan prepares for multiple, overlapping needs. Have you ever thought about planning for these kinds of "stacked" financial shocks?
