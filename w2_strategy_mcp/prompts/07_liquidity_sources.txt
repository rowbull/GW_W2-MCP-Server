# W2 Strategy MCP - Liquidity Sources Prompt

Let's start by understanding what liquidity sources you currently have available. I'm going to ask about a few different categories, and don't worry about being exact - rough estimates are fine.

**Cash and Cash Equivalents:**
How much do you have in checking accounts, savings accounts, and money market accounts combined? This is money you can access immediately with no penalties or tax implications.

**Brokerage Cash:**
Do you keep any cash in your brokerage accounts - cash that's not invested but sitting there available? This is money you can access without having to sell any investments.

**Available Credit:**
What's your available credit across credit cards and lines of credit? I know it sounds counterintuitive to think of debt as liquidity, but available credit can be an important bridge in certain situations.

[IF HOMEOWNER] **Home Equity Line of Credit (HELOC):**
Do you have a HELOC set up? If yes, how much is available to borrow on it right now?

The idea here isn't to judge any particular number - it's to understand your complete liquidity picture so we can see how it matches up with potential needs.

---

## Data Collection Framework:

**Primary Liquidity (Immediate Access):**
- deposits_amount: Checking + savings + money market
- brokerage_cash_amount: Cash sitting in investment accounts

**Secondary Liquidity (Quick Access):**
- available_credit_amount: Credit cards + personal lines of credit
- heloc_amount: Home equity line (if homeowner)

**Notes for MCP Logic:**
- Accept rough estimates ("around $25,000", "maybe $5,000")
- Don't push for exact numbers - this is strategic planning, not accounting
- If they mention liquid securities (bonds, stable ETFs), capture separately
- Focus on money they can access within days, not weeks

## Transition Setup:
After collecting this data: "Good, so you have [TOTAL APPROXIMATE] in various forms of liquidity. Now let's think about what you might actually need that money for..."
