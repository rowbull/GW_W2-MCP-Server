Let's build a quick inventory of your liquidity sources. Think across these categories:

1.  **Immediate Cash**: How much do you have in checking, savings, or money market accounts? This is cash you can access today with no tax implications.
2.  **Available Credit**: What's the total available limit on your credit cards or any lines of credit (like a HELOC)? This is instant access to capital, but it comes with an interest cost.
3.  **Marketable Securities**: Do you have investments in a brokerage account (stocks, bonds, ETFs, mutual funds) that could be sold and converted to cash within a few days? This source can be powerful, but may have tax consequences or require selling at a bad time.

You don't need to share exact numbers, but just a rough sense of each category. For example, "I have about 3 months of expenses in cash, minimal credit card debt, and a decent-sized brokerage account."
